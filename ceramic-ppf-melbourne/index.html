FAQ

Common Questions.

Can you put ceramic over PPF?

Yes — coating over the film adds gloss and hydrophobics and makes the PPF itself easier to clean and maintain.

Do I need PPF on the whole car?

Not usually. Most owners film the high-impact front end and coat the rest — best protection for the money. Full-body PPF is available for those who want it.

Which goes on first?

PPF first, then the ceramic coating over the top of both the film and the bare paint for a uniform finish.
