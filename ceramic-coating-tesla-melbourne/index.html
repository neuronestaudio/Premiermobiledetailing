Why Teslas

Built for Tesla's Soft Paint.

Tesla's water-based factory paint is thinner and softer than almost anything else on the road. That's exactly why a ceramic coating makes such a difference.

Owners notice it within weeks — light swirls from the automatic car wash, marring around the door handles, and wash scratches that show up brutally on Solid Black and Midnight Silver Metallic. Pearl White hides swirls but grabs contamination and water spots instead.

Most Teslas also arrive from delivery with factory imperfections — transport film marks, light swirls and overspray. We correct the paint first, then lock that flawless finish in under a hard ceramic layer so it stays sharp for years, not months.

  • ✓Blocks the UV that fades and oxidises Tesla's thin clear coat
  • ✓Repels water, road grime, salt air and bird droppings
  • ✓Adds serious depth and gloss — especially on black and grey
  • ✓Makes every future wash faster and far safer for soft paint

How We Work

Mobile. Done Right.

We come to you fully equipped. Proper prep is 90% of a coating job — and on soft Tesla paint, it's everything.

Every coating starts with a thorough wash, decontamination and paint correction so the surface is flawless before a drop of coating goes on. A ceramic coating locks in whatever is underneath — so on a Tesla, the correction step matters more than ever.

Coatings need to cure under cover, out of direct sun and rain, so we work in your garage or carport — ideal, since that's where your Tesla already charges. We just need access to a tap and a power point.

FAQ

Tesla Ceramic Questions.

Is ceramic coating worth it on a Tesla?

Yes — arguably more than most cars. Tesla's factory paint is thin and soft, so it swirls and marks easily. Correcting it and locking in that finish under a ceramic coating keeps it sharp for years and makes it far easier to keep clean.

Do you coat brand-new Teslas on delivery?

Absolutely. New Teslas often arrive with transport marks, light swirls or overspray. We correct those first, then coat — so your car is protected and flawless from day one.

Which Teslas do you coat?

All of them — Model 3, Model Y, Model S and Model X. Tell us your model and colour and we'll recommend the right GYEON or ONYX package.

Can you coat the glass roof and wheels too?

Yes. Every package can add glass, wheel-face and interior coatings, matched to the durability of your paint coating. Tesla's panoramic glass roof sheds water and grime beautifully once coated.
