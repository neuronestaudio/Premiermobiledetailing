Two Looks

Invisible or Stealth.

Same protection, very different aesthetic — it comes down to the finish you want.

Gloss PPF

Optically clear film that protects your paint while keeping the exact factory look — often with an even deeper wet-look shine.

Matte PPF

Converts glossy paint to a satin/matte "stealth" finish while adding full stone-chip protection — no respray required.

Same Protection

Both are self-healing and shield against chips and scratches — the only difference is the look.

FAQ

Common Questions.

Can matte PPF make my glossy car matte?

Yes — matte/satin film converts a glossy finish to a stealth look while protecting it, with no respray needed and reversible if you remove the film.

Does gloss PPF add shine?

It keeps the factory look and often deepens the wet-look gloss, especially with a ceramic coating over the top.

Do both protect the same?

Yes — gloss and matte films offer the same stone-chip and self-healing protection; the difference is purely the finish.
