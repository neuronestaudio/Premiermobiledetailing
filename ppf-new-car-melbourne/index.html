Why Film It New

Keep It Chip-Free.

Factory paint is expensive to repair and impossible to truly replace — PPF keeps the original finish flawless.

A single stone chip on a new bonnet is heartbreaking — and touch-ups never quite match. PPF on the front end means the film takes the hit instead of the paint.

Filming early also means the paint underneath stays in perfect condition, which protects resale value and keeps the car looking new for years.

  • ✓Protects factory paint before the first chip
  • ✓Self-healing film keeps the surface swirl-free
  • ✓Preserves resale value and originality
  • ✓Pairs perfectly with a ceramic coating over the rest of the car

FAQ

Common Questions.

Should I get PPF on a brand-new car?

It's ideal — the paint is perfect, so the film locks in a flawless finish and protects it from day one.

Can I combine PPF with ceramic coating?

Yes, and it's a popular combo — PPF on high-impact areas, ceramic over the whole car for gloss and easy cleaning.
