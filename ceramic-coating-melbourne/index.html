The Basics

What a Ceramic Coating Does.

A ceramic coating is a liquid nano-glass layer that chemically bonds to your clear coat, then cures into a hard, slick, hydrophobic shell.

Once cured, it becomes a semi-permanent barrier on top of your factory paint. Water sheets straight off, dirt struggles to stick, and washing takes a fraction of the time.

Melbourne paint takes a beating from every direction — intense Australian UV in summer, salt air off Port Phillip Bay for bayside drivers, bird droppings and tree sap in the leafier South-East suburbs, and hard-water spotting from sprinklers. A quality coating is your defence against all of it.

  • ✓Blocks UV rays that fade and oxidise unprotected clear coat
  • ✓Repels water, road grime, salt and bird droppings
  • ✓Adds serious depth and gloss to the finish
  • ✓Makes every future wash faster and safer for the paint

The Process

Four Stages, One Flawless Finish.

A ceramic coating is only as good as what goes under it — three of these four stages happen before the bottle is even opened.

01

The surface has to be genuinely clean first

Pressure wash to lift loose dirt, a proper hand wash, rinse, then a clay treatment to pull bonded contamination out of the paint. A coating bonds to whatever it is laid on — sealing fallout, rail dust or overspray under it locks that damage in for the life of the coating.

FAQ

Common Questions.

Is ceramic coating worth it in Melbourne?

Yes — arguably more than most places. Australia's UV is brutal, and Melbourne adds coastal salt, hard water and unpredictable weather. A coating keeps your paint sharper for years and makes maintenance easy.

Do you come to me?

We're fully mobile across Officer, Berwick, Pakenham, Cranbourne, Clyde, Narre Warren, Frankston, Dandenong and the wider South-East Melbourne region. Ceramic coatings need to cure under cover, so we work in your garage or carport with access to a tap and power.

How long does it take?

A paint enhancement and ceramic coating is typically a full day. Multi-stage paint correction before a coating can take two days. Spray ceramics are faster.

Which coating should I choose?

It depends on your car, budget and how long you're keeping it. Tell us and we'll recommend the right GYEON or ONYX product — from 12 months up to a lifetime warranty.
