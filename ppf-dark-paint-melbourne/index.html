Dark Paint Is Ruthless

It Shows Everything.

On black and dark colours, a single chip or a web of swirls is glaringly obvious in the sun.

Dark paint is the most rewarding to keep perfect and the most punishing when it's not. Stone chips stand out as white dots, and wash swirls turn into a haze under Melbourne's bright light.

Clear PPF on the high-impact panels stops the chips, and its self-healing top coat keeps the surface swirl-free. Pair it with a ceramic coating and dark paint stays wet-look glossy for years.

  • ✓Stops the stone chips that stand out worst on dark paint
  • ✓Self-healing film keeps the finish swirl-free
  • ✓Optically clear — no effect on the colour
  • ✓Best paired with correction + ceramic for a flawless base

FAQ

Common Questions.

Is PPF worth it on black paint?

Arguably more than any other colour — dark paint shows every chip and swirl, so keeping it protected makes the biggest visible difference.

Will the film change my paint colour?

No — quality gloss PPF is optically clear and, if anything, deepens the wet look, especially with a coating on top.

Should I correct the paint first?

Yes — we correct swirls out first so the film locks in a flawless, mirror-black finish.
