For the Home

Cooler, Comfier, Protected.

Big windows and Melbourne sun make rooms hot and fade your furnishings — window film fixes both.

Residential window film rejects a large share of solar heat, so west-facing rooms stay comfortable in summer and your cooling works less. It also blocks the UV that fades timber floors, rugs, artwork and lounges.

There are options for privacy and security too — from subtle daytime privacy films to safety films that hold glass together if it breaks.

FAQ

Common Questions.

Will home window film make rooms dark?

Not necessarily — modern films reject heat and UV while keeping natural light and the view. There are lighter and darker options to suit each room.

Does it really lower cooling costs?

By rejecting solar heat, film reduces the load on your air-conditioning, especially on west- and north-facing glass.

Can you do privacy film?

Yes — from daytime one-way privacy to frosted films for bathrooms and street-facing windows.
