FAQ

Common Questions.

What does partial PPF cover?

Typically the bonnet, front bumper, guards and mirrors — the front-end panels that take almost all stone-chip damage. Coverage can be tailored to your car.

Is partial PPF enough?

For most daily drivers, yes — it protects the highest-impact areas for the best value. Full-body PPF is there if you want total coverage.

Can I add more later?

Yes — you can start with a front-end package and extend coverage down the track.
