Why Coat Wheels

The Dirtiest Job on the Car.

Hot brake dust bakes onto uncoated wheels and pits the finish over time.

A dedicated wheel coating (like GYEON Q² Rim) is built to handle the heat and the constant assault of brake dust and road salt. Once coated, contaminants sit on the surface instead of bonding to it — so a quick rinse does most of the work.

We coat the faces, and can pull wheels for a full barrel coat on request. It's a natural add-on to any paint coating package.

  • ✓Brake dust and grime rinse off instead of baking on
  • ✓Protects the finish from heat, salt and corrosion
  • ✓Keeps wheels looking new with far less scrubbing
  • ✓Available as an add-on to any ceramic package

FAQ

Common Questions.

Can you coat wheels without removing them?

We coat the wheel faces in place as standard. For a full barrel coat we can remove the wheels — just ask when you book.

Does wheel coating handle brake heat?

Yes — dedicated wheel coatings are formulated for high temperatures, unlike a standard paint coating.

How long does it last?

Typically 1–2 years depending on the product and how the car is driven and washed.
