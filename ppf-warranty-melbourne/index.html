FAQ

Common Questions.

How long is a PPF warranty?

Premium films typically carry multi-year manufacturer warranties against yellowing, cracking and lifting. Exact terms depend on the film — we'll confirm before you book.

What voids a PPF warranty?

Poor installation, damage from abuse or accident, and using the wrong products. Correct professional fitment and normal care keep it valid.

Is the warranty transferable?

In many cases the film's warranty stays with the vehicle — a nice bonus at resale. We'll confirm specifics for your film.
