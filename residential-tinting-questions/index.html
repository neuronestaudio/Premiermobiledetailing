FAQ

Common Questions.

Will window film make my rooms dark?

Not necessarily — modern solar films reject heat and UV while keeping the light and view. There are lighter and darker options per room.

Does home tint reduce power bills?

By cutting solar heat gain, it eases the load on your cooling, especially on west- and north-facing glass in summer.

Does it stop furniture fading?

It blocks the majority of UV, dramatically slowing fading of floors, rugs, artwork and furniture.

Can I get privacy without frosting?

Yes — daytime one-way privacy films let you see out while limiting the view in, without a frosted look.

Does film help in winter too?

Some low-e films help retain heat in winter as well as reject it in summer — we can advise on the right film for your goals.

How long does home film last?

Quality film lasts many years and carries a warranty against bubbling, peeling and excessive fading.
