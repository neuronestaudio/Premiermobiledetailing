The Technology

Marks That Vanish.

Quality PPF uses an elastomeric top coat that "remembers" its original shape.

When the film picks up a light scratch or swirl, warmth — from the sun, warm water or a heat gun — lets the top coat flow back to smooth. The mark literally disappears.

It won't heal a deep gouge, but for the everyday swirls and fine scratches that dull a finish, it keeps the surface looking freshly detailed with no effort.

FAQ

Common Questions.

How does self-healing PPF work?

The film's top coat is heat-reactive — warmth lets it flow back to its original smooth state, erasing light swirls and scratches.

Does it heal deep scratches?

No — deep gouges that cut through the film aren't recoverable. It heals light, surface-level marks.

Does it work in winter?

Yes — warm water or the car's own heat is enough to trigger healing; you don't need a hot day.
