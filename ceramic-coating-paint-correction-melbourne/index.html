Why Correct First

Coat the Perfect Surface.

Ceramic coatings are transparent and semi-permanent — whatever the paint looks like when you coat it is what you lock in for years.

Most cars carry swirl marks from years of incorrect washing, plus etching, water spots and light scratches. Under Melbourne sun those defects are impossible to ignore.

We machine-polish the paint to remove those defects and restore true gloss and clarity, then apply your GYEON or ONYX coating over a corrected, mirror finish. The difference is night and day.

Levels of Correction

One Stage to Multi-Stage.

We match the correction level to your paint and your goals.

Paint Enhancement

A one-stage polish that removes the majority of light swirls and restores gloss — ideal before a coating on a well-kept car.

Multi-Stage Correction

Compounding and refining to remove heavier defects, deeper scratches and oxidation for a near-flawless finish.

Then Coated

Once corrected, we lock the finish in with your chosen ceramic coating so it stays that way for years.

FAQ

Common Questions.

Do I need paint correction before a coating?

Almost always some level. A coating won't hide swirls — it seals them in. At minimum we recommend a one-stage enhancement first.

How long does correction take?

A one-stage enhancement is a few hours; full multi-stage correction can take up to two days depending on the paint.

Can you remove every scratch?

Most swirls and light scratches, yes. Deep scratches that have gone through the clear coat can be improved but not always fully removed.
